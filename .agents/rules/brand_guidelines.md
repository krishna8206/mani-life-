# Brand Guidelines for Mani Lifestyle

- **Indian Brand Identity**: Mani Lifestyle is a premier Indian cosmetics & personal care manufacturer operating from Ahmedabad, Gujarat.
- **Terminology Constraint**: NEVER use the word "turnkey" or "Turnkey B2B Manufacturing Partner".
- **Approved Terminology**: Use terms such as:
  - "Indian B2B Manufacturing Partner"
  - "Contract & Third-Party Manufacturing"
  - "Private Label (OEM / ODM)"
  - "End-to-End Manufacturing Solutions"
